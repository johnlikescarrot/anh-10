# EXAM-01-PART-01 — Sharing housework in An's family

- **Nguồn:** PDF trang 24 (câu hỏi), trang 32 (tapescript), trang 32 (đáp án).
- **Dạng bài:** True/False | **Chủ đề:** Unit 1 – Family Life | **Số từ:** 182.
- **Chỉ dẫn:** Listen to a conversation between An and Mrs. Lan about sharing housework in An's family. Decide whether the following statements are True (A) or False (B).

## Câu hỏi và đáp án

**Question 1.** Both of An's parents work full-time, so all family members share the household chores.

- A. True
- B. False

**Đáp án: A** — bố mẹ làm full-time, cả nhà chia việc

**Question 2.** An's father is only responsible for doing the heavy lifting at weekends.

- A. True
- B. False

**Đáp án: B** — bố không chỉ mang vác nặng cuối tuần mà còn nấu bữa tối từ thứ Hai đến thứ Sáu

**Question 3.** An puts out the rubbish and waters the balcony plants every afternoon.

- A. True
- B. False

**Đáp án: A** — An tưới cây ban công và đổ rác mỗi chiều

**Question 4.** An's younger brother never helps with housework because he is only nine years old.

- A. True
- B. False

**Đáp án: B** — em trai 9 tuổi vẫn cho mèo ăn, gấp quần áo và quét phòng khách

**Question 5.** An feels that sharing chores brings her family closer together rather than making her tired.

- A. True
- B. False

**Đáp án: A** — An thấy gia đình gắn kết hơn chứ không mệt

## Từ khóa cần nghe

`full-time`, `divide household chores equally`, `overloaded`, `heavy lifting`, `grocery shopping`, `laundry`, `put out the rubbish`, `clear the table`, `fold clothes`, `sweep the floor`.

## Bẫy thông tin

`only`, `never`, `full-time`, `AND`, `nine-year-old`, `twenty minutes`.

## Tapescript

```text
Mrs. Lan: Good afternoon, An! Both your parents work full-time at the hospital and an engineering firm. How does your family manage all the housework every day?
An: Good afternoon, Mrs. Lan! In my home, we divide household chores equally so nobody feels overloaded. My dad is a great cook, so he prepares dinner from Monday to Friday AND does the heavy lifting at weekends.
Mrs. Lan: That sounds wonderful! What about your mother and you?
An: My mother does the grocery shopping in the morning and handles the laundry. Every afternoon after school, I water the plants on our balcony and put out the rubbish. After dinner, I also help clear the table.
Mrs. Lan: Does your nine-year-old brother help out too, or is he too young?
An: Oh, he definitely helps! He feeds our cat, folds his own clothes, and sweeps the living room floor every evening.
Mrs. Lan: Aren't you tired after studying all day?
An: Not at all. Spending twenty minutes doing chores together gives us time to chat and laugh, so it really brings our family closer.
```
