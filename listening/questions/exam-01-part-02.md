# EXAM-01-PART-02 — Minh Khoa on Vietnam Melody Star

- **Nguồn:** PDF trang 24 (câu hỏi), trang 32 (tapescript), trang 32 (đáp án).
- **Dạng bài:** MCQs | **Chủ đề:** Unit 3 – Music | **Số từ:** 181.
- **Chỉ dẫn:** Listen to a radio talk about Minh Khoa, a young contestant in a national music show. Choose the best answer A, B, C, or D.

## Câu hỏi và đáp án

**Question 6.** At what age did Minh Khoa start learning to play the acoustic guitar?

- A. At the age of seven
- B. At the age of ten
- C. At the age of twelve
- D. At the age of fifteen

**Đáp án: B** — at the age of ten

**Question 7.** Who taught Minh Khoa his first traditional folk melodies?

- A. His school music teacher
- B. A famous pop judge
- C. His grandfather
- D. His older sister

**Đáp án: C** — his grandfather

**Question 8.** In the quarter-final round, Minh Khoa impressed the audience by combining modern pop with ______.

- A. opera singing
- B. the moon-shaped lute
- C. electronic drums
- D. piano solos

**Đáp án: B** — the moon-shaped lute

**Question 9.** Why was Minh Khoa worried two days before the semi-final night?

- A. He forgot the lyrics of his song.
- B. His guitar string broke on stage.
- C. He caught a mild cold and sore throat.
- D. His parents could not watch the show.

**Đáp án: C** — caught a mild cold and sore throat

**Question 10.** What does Minh Khoa plan to do with his prize money if he wins the competition?

- A. Buy an expensive electric car
- B. Record an album in London
- C. Donate instruments to rural school clubs
- D. Open a private music café

**Đáp án: C** — donate new guitars and flutes to rural high school music clubs

## Từ khóa cần nghe

`finalist`, `acoustic guitar`, `traditional folk tunes`, `quarter-final`, `moon-shaped lute`, `two million views`, `sore throat`, `ginger tea`, `cash prize`, `rural high school music clubs`.

## Bẫy thông tin

## Tapescript

Radio presenter: Welcome back to Youth Spotlight! Tonight, we are talking about Minh Khoa, the sixteen-year-old finalist who has captured millions of hearts on Vietnam Melody Star. Born in a peaceful town in Nghe An, Khoa first picked up an acoustic guitar at the age of ten. Interestingly, his first music teacher was not a professional coach, but his grandfather, who taught him traditional folk tunes every summer evening.
Radio presenter: During last month's quarter-final, Khoa surprised all three judges by blending a modern pop ballad with the sound of the moon-shaped lute. Video clips of that live performance quickly reached two million views online. However, his journey has not been easy. Just two days before the semi-final night, Khoa caught a mild cold and suffered from a sore throat. Thanks to warm ginger tea and careful rest, he recovered in time to sing brilliantly.
Radio presenter: In a recent interview, Khoa shared a heartwarming plan: if he wins the top cash prize this Sunday, he will donate new guitars and flutes to rural high school music clubs so country teenagers can pursue their musical dreams too.
