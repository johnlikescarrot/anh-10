# EXAM-02-PART-01 — The Eco-Campus Fair announcement

- **Nguồn:** PDF trang 27 (câu hỏi), trang 33 (tapescript), trang 33 (đáp án).
- **Dạng bài:** True/False | **Chủ đề:** Unit 2 – Humans and the Environment | **Số từ:** 186.
- **Chỉ dẫn:** Listen to an announcement by Mai, leader of the Go Green Youth Club, about an upcoming school event. Decide whether the statements are True (A) or False (B).

## Câu hỏi và đáp án

**Question 1.** The Eco-Campus Fair will take place in the main schoolyard this Saturday morning.

- A. True
- B. False

**Đáp án: A** — tổ chức ở sân trường chính sáng thứ Bảy

**Question 2.** Students need to buy entrance tickets for 20,000 VND to join the workshops.

- A. True
- B. False

**Đáp án: B** — vào cửa hoàn toàn miễn phí completely free, không tốn 20.000đ

**Question 3.** Visitors can bring used plastic bottles to exchange for small notebooks or tree saplings.

- A. True
- B. False

**Đáp án: A** — đổi vỏ chai nhựa lấy sổ tái chế hoặc cây non

**Question 4.** Grade 10C is going to perform a short play about ocean pollution at 10:30 a.m.

- A. True
- B. False

**Đáp án: B** — vở kịch diễn lúc 9:30 a.m., không phải 10:30 a.m. — bẫy giờ kinh điển

**Question 5.** The main goal of the fair is to encourage students to practice green habits every day.

- A. True
- B. False

**Đáp án: A** — khuyến khích biến hành động xanh thành thói quen hằng ngày

## Từ khóa cần nghe

`announcement`, `annual Eco-Campus Fair`, `main schoolyard`, `admission`, `display booth`, `hands-on workshop`, `aluminum cans`, `recycled paper notebook`, `young green sapling`, `ocean plastic pollution`, `acoustic music mini-show`.

## Bẫy thông tin

`completely free`, `ten clean used bottles`, `9:30 a.m., not 10:30`, `20,000 VND`, `daily habits`.

## Tapescript

```text
Mai: Good morning, everyone! I'm Mai, leader of our school's Go Green Youth Club, and I have an exciting announcement for this weekend. This Saturday morning, from 7:30 a.m. to 11:30 a.m., our annual Eco-Campus Fair is going to take place right in the main schoolyard.
Mai: Best of all, admission to every display booth and hands-on workshop is completely free for all students, parents, and teachers, you don't need to buy any entrance tickets at all! Remember to check your home carefully before coming: if you bring ten clean used plastic bottles or aluminum cans to our Green Booth, you can exchange them for a recycled paper notebook or a young green sapling.
Mai: At 9:30 a.m., not 10:30, talented students from Grade 10C will perform a funny fifteen-minute play about protecting sea turtles from ocean plastic pollution, followed by an acoustic music mini-show. Through this meaningful fair, our club hopes to inspire everyone to turn small green actions into daily habits both at school and at home. Bring your close friends and reusable water bottles, see you all this Saturday!
```
