EXAM-02-PART-02 — Family mealtime routines with Dr. Hoang Minh
Source PDF: p.34 | 187 words | MCQs | Unit 1 – Family Life
Speakers: Host, Dr. Minh
==============================================================================

Host: Welcome to Family Matters! Today Dr. Hoang Minh is sharing findings from his recent survey on urban family meals. Dr. Minh, how often do city families eat dinner together nowadays?
Dr. Minh: Thank you! Because of evening classes and overtime work, busy city families in our survey share dinner only about four evenings a week on average.
Host: And when they do sit down together, what gets in the way of conversation?
Dr. Minh: Definitely smartphones and television screens. When everyone stares at notifications or video clips, family members sit at the same table yet barely talk to one another.
Host: How can tenth-grade teenagers help make family meals warmer?
Dr. Minh: Teenagers don't need to cook complicated feasts. Simply washing vegetables and setting the table before dinner shows care and gratitude. Our study also found that teenagers who chat regularly with their parents over dinner develop stronger communication skills and experience much lower school stress.
Host: What is one golden rule every household can start tonight?
Dr. Minh: Keep the dinner table completely "screen-free" for just thirty minutes. That half hour builds lifelong family bonds!
