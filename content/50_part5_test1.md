>. Music has been part of human life for thousands of years, but until recently, scientists did not know exactly how it affects the brain. This explains why a song from our childhood can bring back a memory so clearly.

### Bài viết mẫu – Câu 35 (141 từ)

> In my opinion, sharing the housework brings several benefits to every family. Firstly, it teaches children essential life skills. When I learned to cook and do the laundry, I became more independent, and I now feel confident about living on my own in the future. Secondly, sharing chores reduces the burden on parents. Both of my parents work long hours, so when my brother and I tidy up the house, they have more time to rest and relax. Finally, doing the housework together makes the family closer. Every Sunday, we clean the house as a team and then have lunch together, and those are the moments when we talk and laugh the most. For these reasons, I strongly believe that all members of a family should share the housework, because a shared home is a happy home.
