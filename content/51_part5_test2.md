### Part 2. Read the passage and choose the best answer. (1,25 điểm)

>. Every year, millions of tonnes of plastic end up in the ocean. Plastic bags, bottles and straws are thrown away after a single use, and many of them drift for hundreds of years before they break down. Sea animals often mistake this rubbish for food: turtles eat plastic bags because they look like jellyfish, and seabirds feed small pieces of plastic to their babies.
>
> The problem is not only in the ocean. In Vietnam, the amount of household waste has risen quickly in recent years, especially in big cities. Most of it is buried in landfills, which take up space and can pollute the soil and the water. Burning rubbish is another common solution, but it releases harmful gases into the air.
>
> Fortunately, there are simple ways for each family to help. First, we can refuse single-use plastic and carry a reusable bag or bottle. Second, we can separate our waste: paper, plastic, glass and food waste should go into different bins. Third, we can buy products that are made from recycled materials. Experts say that if every household followed these three steps, the amount of waste going to landfills could fall by nearly half.
>
> Change begins at home. If each of us makes one small habit greener today, our children will inherit a cleaner and safer planet tomorrow.

### Bài viết mẫu – Câu 35 (144 từ)

> Students can protect the environment by changing a few simple daily habits. Firstly, we should refuse single-use plastic. I always carry a reusable water bottle and a cloth bag, and I say no to plastic straws when I buy drinks. Secondly, we should sort our rubbish into paper, plastic, glass and food waste so that it can be recycled or reused instead of being buried in landfills. Thirdly, we can take part in school activities. Our Go Green Club organises a clean-up along the river every month, and we also plant trees in the school yard. Finally, we should encourage our friends and families to do the same by sharing simple tips on social media. In my opinion, protecting the environment does not require great actions from a few people; it requires small actions from millions of people, repeated every day.

>+ **Nhận xét**: dùng 5 từ nối (*Firstly, Secondly, Thirdly, Finally, In my opinion*), 6 từ vựng chủ đề (*single-use plastic, reusable, sort, recycled, clean-up, landfills*), 144 từ – nằm đúng vùng an toàn.

### KỊCH BẢN NGHE – ĐỀ SỐ 2

>. **Part 1** (~190 từ)
> Hi everyone, my name is Nam and I'm a member of the Go Green Club at my school. I'd like to tell you about our next activity. This year our clean-up day will take place on Sunday, the twelfth of October. We are going to collect plastic bottles and rubbish along the river near our school, and we will also plant fifty small trees. Volunteers should meet at the school gate at eight a.m., not at the river, because we will walk there together in groups. The activity will finish at about eleven thirty. Last year, more than two hundred students joined us and we collected over one tonne of rubbish, so we hope to do even better this time. Don't worry about food: the club will provide a free lunch for every volunteer. Just bring a pair of gloves, a hat and a reusable water bottle. If you want to join, please register with your class monitor before Friday.
