# PHẦN 5 — KỸ NĂNG VIẾT (30% TỔNG ĐIỂM)

Phần Viết gồm **3 bài** với tổng **3,0 điểm**: sắp xếp câu (0,5) – viết lại câu (1,0) – viết đoạn (1,5). Đây là phần **dễ mất điểm nhất nhưng cũng dễ lấy điểm nhất** nếu thuộc công thức.

## 5.1. Writing 1 — Sắp xếp câu thành đoạn văn / lá thư / hội thoại

### 5.1.1. Bốn "la bàn" tìm câu mở đầu và thứ tự

| Dấu hiệu | Nói cho em biết |
|---|---|
| **Lời chào, Dear…, Hi…, Kính gửi** | Luôn là **câu đầu tiên** |
| **Từ nối thời gian**: First, Then, After that, Next, Finally | Thứ tự tăng dần: First → … → Finally |
| **Đại từ thay thế**: he, she, it, this, these, they | Phải đứng **sau** danh từ mà nó thay thế |
| **Mạo từ**: a/an (lần đầu) → the (lần sau) | *a boy* xuất hiện trước, *the boy* xuất hiện sau |
| **Câu hỏi – câu trả lời** | Trong hội thoại: hỏi trước, đáp sau |
| **Câu kết**: In short, Therefore, I look forward to hearing from you, Best wishes | Luôn ở **cuối** |

> **MẸO 10 GIÂY:** Tìm câu **đầu** và câu **cuối** trước (chiếm 2/4 đáp án đúng), phần giữa thường tự sáng tỏ.

### 5.1.2. Bài tập sắp xếp (5 bài)

**Bài 1 — Lá thư. Sắp xếp các câu sau thành lá thư hoàn chỉnh.**

> a. First, I help my mother to prepare breakfast and wash the dishes.
> b. Dear Tom, I'm writing to tell you about my daily routine.
> c. In the evening, my sister and I take turns to walk the dog.
> d. Finally, we all go to bed before eleven o'clock.
> e. After school, I usually tidy up my room and water the plants.

> **ĐÁP ÁN: b – a – e – c – d**
> **Vì sao:** b mở đầu (lời chào + mục đích thư) → a (*First*) → e (*After school*) → c (*In the evening*) → d (*Finally*).

**Bài 2 — Hội thoại. Sắp xếp thành đoạn hội thoại hợp lý.**

> a. That's a great idea. What kind of music does she like?
> b. Yes, I'd love to. When is it?
> c. Would you like to go to the concert with me on Saturday?
> d. She loves pop music, especially the songs of Son Tung M-TP.
> e. It starts at eight o'clock. Let's meet at the gate at half past seven.

> **ĐÁP ÁN: c – b – a – d – e**
> **Vì sao:** c là lời mời → b trả lời + hỏi giờ → a đồng ý + hỏi tiếp → d trả lời về thể loại nhạc → e trả lời về giờ. *Chú ý: e trả lời câu hỏi "When is it?" nhưng phải đứng sau vì a hỏi thêm một câu khác; mạch tự nhiên là hỏi–đáp theo cặp.*

**Bài 3 — Đoạn văn về môi trường.**

> a. Second, we turn off the lights when we leave a room.
> b. There are several simple ways my family protects the environment.
> c. In short, small actions can make a big difference.
> d. Third, we bring our own bottles instead of buying plastic ones.
> e. First, we sort our rubbish into paper, plastic and food waste.

> **ĐÁP ÁN: b – e – a – d – c**
> **Vì sao:** b là **câu chủ đề** → e (*First*) → a (*Second*) → d (*Third*) → c (*In short* = kết luận).

**Bài 4 — Đoạn văn về âm nhạc.**

> a. I usually listen to music while I do my homework.
> b. Music plays an important part in my life.
> c. It helps me relax and concentrate better.
> d. In the evening, I sometimes play the guitar with my father.
> e. For these reasons, I cannot imagine my life without music.

> **ĐÁP ÁN: b – a – c – d – e**
> **Vì sao:** b câu chủ đề → a nêu thói quen → c giải thích *It* (= music) → d thêm hoạt động buổi tối → e kết luận.

**Bài 5 — Hội thoại về việc nhà.**

> a. Not really. I only take out the rubbish and tidy my room.
> b. Do you have to do a lot of housework, Nam?
> c. That's not too bad. I have to wash the dishes every day.
> d. Yes, but my sister helps me, so it's quick.
> e. Oh, that sounds tiring. Does it take you a long time?

> **ĐÁP ÁN: b – a – c – e – d**
> **Vì sao:** b mở đầu bằng câu hỏi → a trả lời → c nhận xét + nêu việc của mình → e hỏi lại → d trả lời.

## 5.2. Writing 2 — Viết lại câu (Sentence transformation)

Bản đặc tả yêu cầu: *"Sử dụng kiến thức về cấu trúc câu để viết lại câu cùng nghĩa với câu đã cho."* Chỉ có **5 công thức** dưới đây là đủ dùng — học thuộc lòng từng công thức.

### 5.2.1. Năm công thức biến đổi

| # | Dạng | Công thức | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Chủ động → Bị động** | S + V + O → **O + be + V3 (+ by S)** | *They built this school in 1990.* → *This school **was built** in 1990.* |
| 2 | **Hai câu đơn → Câu ghép** | Dùng **and / but / so / or / yet** | *It rained. We stayed at home.* → *It rained, **so** we stayed at home.* |
| 3 | **Dự định → be going to** | intend / plan to V → **be going to V** | *My father intends to buy a guitar.* → *My father **is going to buy** a guitar.* |
| 4 | **too … to / enough to / It is + adj + to V** | S + be + too + adj + to V ⇄ S + be + so + adj + that … | *He is too young to drive.* ⇄ *He is so young that he can't drive.* |
| 5 | **make / let → bị động** | make sb do → **be made to do**; let sb do → **be allowed to do** | *They made him clean the floor.* → *He **was made to clean** the floor.* |

> **CẢNH BÁO:** Khi viết lại câu, **không đổi thì** của câu gốc và **không thêm thông tin mới**. Chỉ đổi **cấu trúc**. Sai thì = mất điểm cả câu.

### 5.2.2. Bài tập viết lại câu (20 câu, có đáp án)

1. My mother waters the plants every morning.
   → The plants ______________________________________
2. The students clean the classroom every day.
   → The classroom ___________________________________
3. Someone built this school in 1990.
   → This school _____________________________________
4. They will organise the concert next month.
   → The concert _____________________________________
5. They have planted thousands of trees since 2010.
   → Thousands of trees ______________________________
6. The teacher made him clean the classroom.
   → He _____________________________________________
7. People say that music reduces stress.
   → It ______________________________________________
8. He wanted to go to the concert. He had to study for the exam. (but)
   → ________________________________________________
9. It was raining heavily. We stayed at home. (so)
   → ________________________________________________
10. Turn off the lights. You will waste electricity. (or)
    → ________________________________________________
11. She loves folk music. Her brother prefers rock. (but)
    → ________________________________________________
12. My father intends to buy a new guitar next week.
    → My father ______________________________________
13. We plan to plant more trees in the school garden.
    → We _____________________________________________
14. Living in an extended family is very beneficial.
    → It is ___________________________________________
15. Sharing housework is good for children.
    → It is ___________________________________________
16. He can't play in the band because he is too young.
    → He is too young _________________________________
17. She is so young that she can't drive a car.
    → She is too young ________________________________
18. You shouldn't use plastic bags.
    → Plastic bags ____________________________________
19. My parents let me choose my own clothes.
    → I am ___________________________________________
20. Nobody told me about the meeting.
    → I ______________________________________________

> **ĐÁP ÁN:**
> 1. The plants **are watered by my mother every morning.**
> 2. The classroom **is cleaned by the students every day.**
> 3. This school **was built in 1990.**
> 4. The concert **will be organised next month.**
> 5. Thousands of trees **have been planted since 2010.**
> 6. He **was made to clean the classroom (by the teacher).**
> 7. It **is said that music reduces stress.**
> 8. He wanted to go to the concert, **but he had to study for the exam.**
> 9. It was raining heavily, **so we stayed at home.**
> 10. Turn off the lights, **or you will waste electricity.**
> 11. She loves folk music, **but her brother prefers rock.**
> 12. My father **is going to buy a new guitar next week.**
> 13. We **are going to plant more trees in the school garden.**
> 14. It is **very beneficial to live in an extended family.**
> 15. It is **good for children to share housework.**
> 16. He is too young **to play in the band.**
> 17. She is too young **to drive a car.**
> 18. Plastic bags **shouldn't be used.**
> 19. I am **allowed to choose my own clothes (by my parents).**
> 20. I **wasn't told about the meeting.**

### 5.2.3. Bảng lỗi thường gặp khi viết lại câu

| Lỗi | Ví dụ sai | Sửa đúng |
|---|---|---|
| Quên **be** trong bị động | *This school built in 1990.* | *This school **was** built in 1990.* |
| Quên **to** sau *be made* | *He was made clean the floor.* | *He was made **to** clean the floor.* |
| Dùng *that* thay vì *to* | *It is good that children share…* | *It is good **for children to share**…* |
| Đổi thì câu gốc | *They will organise…* → *The concert **was** organised…* | giữ nguyên **will be organised** |
| Viết lại y nguyên câu gốc | *He is too young to drive.* → *He is too young to drive.* | phải **đổi cấu trúc** |

## 5.3. Writing 3 — Viết đoạn văn 120–150 từ (1,5 điểm)

### 5.3.1. Khung "3 phần – 5 câu"

| Phần | Chức năng | Mẫu câu |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Câu chủ đề** (1 câu) | Nêu ý chính | *In my opinion, … / There are several reasons why … / … plays an important part in my life.* |
| **2. Thân đoạn** (3–5 câu) | 3 ý, mỗi ý 1–2 câu | *First, … Second, … Third, … / Moreover, … / In addition, …* |
| **3. Câu kết** (1 câu) | Khẳng định lại | *In short, … / For these reasons, … / I believe that …* |

**Công thức vàng: 1 + 3 + 1 = 5 câu ≈ 120–150 từ.** Trung bình mỗi câu 22–25 từ là vừa đủ.

### 5.3.2. Kho từ nối "ăn điểm"

| Chức năng | Từ nối |
|---|---|
| Liệt kê | first, second, third, finally, first of all, next |
| Bổ sung | moreover, in addition, besides, also, furthermore |
| Tương phản | however, on the other hand, although, nevertheless |
| Nguyên nhân | because, since, as, due to |
| Kết quả | therefore, as a result, so, consequently |
| Kết luận | in short, in conclusion, for these reasons, to sum up |
| Ý kiến cá nhân | in my opinion, I believe, from my point of view, personally |

### 5.3.3. Ba bài viết mẫu (đếm từ chuẩn)

**Đề 1: Write a paragraph (120–150 words) about the household chores you do and how your family shares them.**

> In my family, everyone shares the household chores. I am responsible for taking out the rubbish every evening and doing the washing-up after dinner. My younger sister waters the plants and feeds our dog, while my mother does most of the cooking. My father handles the heavy lifting, such as cleaning the windows and repairing things around the house. On Saturdays, we all spend about two hours cleaning the house together. At first, I thought doing chores was boring and tiring. However, I have learned to do many things by myself, and I feel proud when the house looks tidy. Sharing chores also gives us time to talk and laugh together. In my opinion, every family member should do their part because a clean home is everyone's responsibility. *(≈135 words)*

**Đề 2: Write a paragraph (120–150 words) about what you and your family do to protect the environment.**

> Protecting the environment starts with small actions at home. In my family, we do three simple things every day. First, we sort our rubbish into plastic, paper and food waste so that it can be recycled more easily. Second, we never use single-use plastic bags; my mother always brings cloth bags when she goes shopping. Third, we turn off the lights and unplug electrical devices when we do not use them, which saves both energy and money. We also grow some vegetables on our balcony, so we do not need to buy vegetables wrapped in plastic. These actions may seem small, but if every family did the same, the result would be huge. I believe that protecting the environment is not only the job of governments; it is the duty of each person. *(≈140 words)*

**Đề 3: Write a paragraph (120–150 words) about your favourite kind of music and why you like it.**

> Music plays an important part in my life. My favourite genre is pop, especially songs with meaningful lyrics and a catchy melody. I usually listen to music while I am doing my homework or travelling to school, because it helps me relax and concentrate. My favourite singer is a young Vietnamese artist whose songs are about friendship and dreams. I admire her not only because she has a beautiful voice but also because she works hard to help young people in poor areas. Last year, I joined the school choir, and singing with my friends made me much more confident. For me, music is more than entertainment. It is a way to express feelings and to connect with other people. *(≈130 words)*

### 5.3.4. Thang điểm viết đoạn (1,5 điểm) và checklist tự chấm

| Tiêu chí | Điểm | Tự kiểm tra |
|---|---|---|
| **Nội dung** (đúng chủ đề, đủ 3 ý, có quan điểm) | 0,5 | ☐ Đã trả lời đúng đề? |
| **Tổ chức** (câu chủ đề – thân – kết, từ nối) | 0,5 | ☐ Có ít nhất 3 từ nối? |
| **Ngôn ngữ** (ngữ pháp, từ vựng theo 6 chuyên đề) | 0,4 | ☐ Không có lỗi chia thì, số ít/số nhiều? |
| **Độ dài & hình thức** (120–150 từ, viết hoa, dấu câu) | 0,1 | ☐ Đếm đủ từ chưa? |

> **BỐN LỖI KHIẾN BÀI VIẾT BỊ TRỪ NHIỀU NHẤT:**
> 1. Không có **câu chủ đề** → giám khảo không biết đoạn văn nói về gì.
> 2. Dùng **câu quá ngắn** liên tiếp (*I like music. It is good. I listen every day.*) → hãy nối bằng *because, so, and*.
> 3. **Sai chính tả** các từ cơ bản: *enviroment* ✗ → *environment* ✓; *recieve* ✗ → *receive* ✓.
> 4. **Không viết hoa** đầu câu hoặc sau dấu chấm → mất điểm hình thức.

### 5.3.5. Khung dự phòng cho mọi đề (dùng khi "bí")

> **Mở:** *… is an important part of my life / a serious problem today / something I care about.*
> **Thân:** *First, … Second, … Third, …* (mỗi ý 1 ví dụ hoặc 1 lý do).
> **Kết:** *In short, I believe that …*

Chỉ cần **thay 3 ý ở phần thân**, khung này dùng được cho cả ba chủ đề của Unit 1, 2, 3.
