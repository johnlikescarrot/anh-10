## 4.3. Vocabulary

### Bài tập 1 – Chọn từ đúng nhất (12 câu)

1. My mother usually ____ the laundry every Saturday morning.
A. makes
B. does
C. takes
D. gets

2. In my family, we all ____ the housework equally.
A. share
B. divide
C. split
D. give

3. My father is the ____ of the family; he earns most of the money.
A. homemaker
B. housekeeper
C. breadwinner
D. caregiver

4. The ____ between my parents and me is sometimes difficult to bridge.
A. upbringing
B. nuclear family
C. extended family
D. generation gap

5. We should ____ the amount of plastic we use every day.
A. raise
B. reduce
C. release
D. replace

6. Many animals are now in danger of ____ because their natural habitats are being destroyed.
A. conservation
B. survival
C. extinction
D. evolution

7. Renewable sources of energy are more ____ because they will never run out.
A. sustainable
B. severe
C. harmful
D. temporary

8. The club is launching a ____ to raise awareness of water pollution.
A. ceremony
B. concert
C. competition
D. campaign

9. She has a real ____ for music – she can play three instruments.
A. hobby
B. talent
C. habit
D. interest

10. The band will ____ their new album at the end of this month.
A. release
B. compose
C. perform
D. record

11. His song became a ____ and stayed at number one for six weeks.
A. beat
B. tune
C. hit
D. rhythm

12. We should ____ our traditional folk music for future generations.
A. protest
B. prevent
C. pretend
D. preserve

**Đáp án:** [[KEY 1B 2A 3C 4D 5B 6C 7A 8D 9B 10A 11C 12D]]

>+ **Giải thích nhanh**
> 1. **do the laundry** (không dùng *make*). 2. **share the housework**. 3. **breadwinner** = người kiếm tiền chính. 4. **generation gap** + *bridge* (thu hẹp). 5. **reduce** = giảm. 6. **in danger of extinction** = có nguy cơ tuyệt chủng. 7. **sustainable** = bền vững. 8. **launch a campaign** = phát động chiến dịch. 9. **have a talent for**. 10. **release an album** = phát hành. 11. **a hit** = bài hát ăn khách. 12. **preserve** = gìn giữ.

### Bài tập 2 – Cho dạng đúng của từ trong ngoặc (12 câu)

1. Air ____ (POLLUTE) in big cities is getting worse every year.
2. The ____ (PROTECT) of endangered animals is our shared responsibility.
3. Deforestation is one of the main causes of the ____ (DESTROY) of natural habitats.
4. We need to use water and electricity more ____ (EFFECT).
5. Her ____ (PERFORM) at the school concert last night was amazing.
6. He is a talented ____ (COMPOSE) who has written more than one hundred songs.
7. My parents always ____ (COURAGE) me to try new things.
8. Sharing the housework helps children become more ____ (DEPEND).
9. The ____ (RESPONSIBLE) for household chores should be shared by all members.
10. ____ (TRADITION) folk music is an important part of our culture.
11. The ____ (MUSIC) played the violin so beautifully that everyone stood up.
12. We must raise public ____ (AWARE) of environmental problems.

**Đáp án:** 1. pollution · 2. protection · 3. destruction · 4. effectively · 5. performance · 6. composer · 7. encourage · 8. independent · 9. responsibility · 10. Traditional · 11. musician · 12. awareness

>+ **Giải thích nhanh**
> 1. *Air ____ is* → cần **danh từ** không đếm được → pollution. 2. *The ____ of* → danh từ → protection. 3. *the ____ of* → danh từ → destruction. 4. bổ nghĩa cho *use* → **trạng từ** → effectively. 5. sau *Her* → danh từ → performance. 6. sau *a talented* → danh từ chỉ người → composer. 7. thì hiện tại đơn, chủ ngữ số nhiều → động từ nguyên mẫu → encourage. 8. sau *more* + *become* → tính từ → independent. 9. *The ____ for* → danh từ → responsibility. 10. đứng đầu câu, bổ nghĩa cho *folk music* → tính từ → Traditional (viết hoa). 11. *The ____ played* → danh từ chỉ người → musician. 12. *public ____* → danh từ → awareness.

## 4.4. Grammar (16 câu trắc nghiệm)

1. My sister ____ the washing-up every evening, but today she ____ it because she is ill.
A. does / doesn't do
B. is doing / doesn't do
C. does / isn't doing
D. is doing / isn't doing

2. Listen! Someone ____ the piano in the next room.
A. is playing
B. plays
C. play
D. has played

3. I ____ this grammar point – could you explain it again?
A. am not understanding
B. don't understand
C. am not understand
D. doesn't understand

4. A: The phone is ringing. B: OK, I ____ it.
A. answer
B. am going to answer
C. am answering
D. will answer

5. Look at those dark clouds! It ____.
A. is going to rain
B. will rain
C. rains
D. would rain

6. They ____ a new swimming pool in our school next year – the plan was approved last month.
A. build
B. are going to build
C. will build
D. are building

7. The classroom ____ by the students every afternoon.
A. cleans
B. is cleaning
C. is cleaned
D. was cleaning

8. A new bridge ____ over the river at the moment.
A. has built
B. is building
C. is built
D. is being built

9. People believe that music ____ stress and improves mood.
A. reduces
B. is reduced
C. reducing
D. reduce

10. It ____ that the concert was cancelled because of the storm.
A. says
B. is said
C. saying
D. said

11. My mother does the cooking, ____ my father does the washing-up.
A. because
B. so
C. and
D. although

12. Although the song is quite simple, ____.
A. and it is very moving
B. so it is very moving
C. but it is very moving
D. it is very moving

13. My parents let me ____ my own hobby.
A. choose
B. choosing
C. to choose
D. chose

14. She decided ____ the Go Green Club because she cares about the environment.
A. join
B. joining
C. joined
D. to join

15. The teacher made the students ____ the poem by heart.
A. to learn
B. learn
C. learning
D. learnt

16. It took us three hours ____ the whole house before Tet.
A. clean
B. cleaning
C. to clean
D. cleaned

**Đáp án:** [[KEY 1C 2A 3B 4D 5A 6B 7C 8D 9A 10B 11C 12D 13A 14D 15B 16C]]

>+ **Giải thích nhanh**
> 1. *every evening* → hiện tại đơn; *today* → tiếp diễn (tạm thời) → **does / isn't doing**.
> 2. *Listen!* → đang xảy ra. 3. *understand* là động từ trạng thái → **không** tiếp diễn.
> 4. Quyết định ngay lúc nói → **will**. 5. Có bằng chứng trước mắt (*dark clouds*) → **be going to**.
> 6. Kế hoạch đã được duyệt → **be going to**. 7 – 8. Bị động: *is cleaned* / *is being built*.
> 9 – 10. Chủ động đúng thì (*reduces*); bị động khách quan *It is said that…*.
> 11. Hai mệnh đề độc lập, cùng chiều → **and**. 12. *Although* → **không** dùng *but/so*.
> 13, 15. **let / make** + O + **V-bare**. 14. **decide** + **to V**. 16. **It takes** + O + time + **to V**.

>! **Ba câu dễ sai nhất trong bài này là 1, 6, 12.** Nếu sai câu 12, hãy đọc lại mục 2.7; sai câu 6, đọc lại mục 2.5; sai câu 1, đọc lại mục 2.4.
