## 4.1. Listening 1 – True/False

>. **Cách luyện**: mở phần **Kịch bản nghe** ở cuối mục 4.2, nhờ bạn/người thân đọc hoặc dùng ứng dụng chuyển văn bản thành giọng nói (tốc độ 0,9×), nghe **2 lần** rồi làm bài. Không nhìn kịch bản khi làm.

### Bài nghe 1 – Chủ đề Family life

Nghe và quyết định các phát biểu sau là **True (T)** hay **False (F)**.

1. Linda's family has four members.
2. Linda's mother is the main breadwinner of the family.
3. Linda's father never does the cooking.
4. Linda and her brother take turns doing the washing-up.
5. The family usually does the housework together on Sunday morning.

**Đáp án:** [[KEY 1F 2T 3F 4T 5T]]

### Bài nghe 2 – Chủ đề Music

1. The speaker's favourite kind of music is classical music.
2. The speaker started learning the guitar when she was ten.
3. Her first performance was at a school concert.
4. She finds folk music boring and never listens to it.
5. She thinks music can help people feel less stressed.

**Đáp án:** [[KEY 1F 2F 3T 4F 5T]]

### Kịch bản nghe (Transcripts) – dùng để luyện

>. **Bài 1 – Family life** (~185 từ)
>. Hi, my name's Linda and today I'd like to talk about how my family shares the housework. There are five of us: my parents, my grandmother, my younger brother Peter and me. My mother is a doctor and she's the main breadwinner, so she often works long hours. My father is a teacher, and he is in charge of the cooking – he's actually a very good cook! My grandmother looks after the garden and waters the plants every morning. Peter and I take turns doing the washing-up: I do it on Monday, Wednesday and Friday, and he does it on the other days. We also have a rule that everyone tidies up their own room before breakfast. On Sunday morning, we all spend two hours cleaning the house together, and after that we usually go out for lunch. I think sharing the housework makes us a closer family, and it teaches us to be responsible.

>. **Bài 2 – Music** (~180 từ)
>. Hello everyone. My name is Hoa and I'm going to talk about the music I love. Most of my friends are crazy about pop and hip-hop, but my favourite kind of music is folk music, especially the traditional songs of my region. I started learning the guitar when I was twelve, not ten, because my parents gave me one for my twelfth birthday. My first performance was at a school concert in grade nine, and I was so nervous that I forgot the lyrics twice! Many people think folk music is boring, but I completely disagree. For me, the melodies are beautiful and the lyrics tell stories about ordinary people, so I never get tired of it. I also believe music is good for us: when I feel stressed before an exam, I play the guitar for twenty minutes and I always feel calmer afterwards. That's why I think every student should learn at least one musical instrument.

>. **Bài 3 – Environment** (~190 từ)
>. **Boy**: Hi Mai, have you heard about the clean-up event this weekend?
>. **Girl**: Not yet. What is it exactly?
>. **Boy**: The Go Green Club is organising a clean-up along the river near our school. We'll collect plastic bottles and rubbish from the riverbank.
>. **Girl**: That sounds useful. Where do we meet?
>. **Boy**: At the school gate, not at the river, because we'll walk there together.
>. **Girl**: OK. What time does it start?
>. **Boy**: The event starts at half past seven. Please arrive ten minutes earlier so we can check your name.
>. **Girl**: Sure. What should I bring?
>. **Boy**: Just a pair of gloves and a big bag. The club will provide water, so you don't need to bring any.
>. **Girl**: Great. How many students have signed up?
>. **Boy**: Twenty-five so far, but we hope to reach thirty-five by Friday.
>. **Girl**: Excellent. I'll ask my brother to join too.

>. **Bài 4 – Music** (~185 từ)
>. **Girl**: Hi Minh, it's Lan. I'm calling about the music concert on Saturday. Would you like to come with me?
>. **Boy**: Oh, which concert is it?
>. **Girl**: It's a live show with several Vietnamese singers, and the money from the tickets goes to a charity for children.
>. **Boy**: That sounds interesting. What kind of music will they play?
>. **Girl**: Mostly pop and some folk songs. I know you prefer rock, but I think you'll enjoy it.
>. **Boy**: Alright, I'll give it a try. When does it start?
>. **Girl**: At seven in the evening on Saturday. It finishes at about nine thirty.
>. **Boy**: And how much is a ticket?
>. **Girl**: One hundred and fifty thousand dong each. I'm going to buy them online tonight.
>. **Boy**: Could you buy one for me too? I need to ask my parents first, though. I'll call you back in an hour.
>. **Girl**: No problem. Talk to you soon.
