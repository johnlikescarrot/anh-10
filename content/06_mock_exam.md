### 6.2.2. Giải thích những câu dễ sai

| Câu | Vì sao đúng | Bẫy nằm ở đâu |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Sister **cannot** iron (she is only 14) → **F** | Nghe thấy *laundry* dễ tưởng là đúng |
| 4 | Grandparents **live with** Nam's family → **F** | *"They live with us"* |
| 7 | *twenty-four bands, six more than last year* → **24** | Bẫy số *six* |
| 9 | *recording video is not allowed* nhưng **được chụp ảnh** (tắt flash) → **B** | Câu phủ định một nửa |
| 11 | **do the heavy lifting** là collocation cố định | *make* dễ bị chọn vì nghĩ "làm" |
| 13 | **pollution** — sau *air* cần danh từ | Dễ viết *polluted* (tính từ) |
| 14 | **talented** — bổ nghĩa cho *singer* | Dễ viết *talent* (danh từ) |
| 15 | *Be quiet!* → hành động đang diễn ra → **is sleeping** | Chủ ngữ *the baby* số ít, dễ chọn *sleeps* |
| 16 | **let + O + V** (không "to") | Nhầm với *allow sb to V* |
| 17 | *Look at those clouds!* = bằng chứng hiện tại → **is going to rain** | *will rain* là bẫy số 1 |
| 18 | *two years ago* → quá khứ đơn + bị động → **was rebuilt** | Thiếu *be* là lỗi phổ biến |
| 21 | Người chấm thi = **judges** | *contestants* là thí sinh |
| 23 | **miss the deadline** = trễ hạn | *lose* không đi với *deadline* |

| 27 | Bài nêu **vấn đề (đoạn 1–2) + giải pháp (đoạn 3–4)** → **A** | Câu B chỉ là chi tiết nửa đầu đoạn 1 |

### 6.2.3. Bài viết mẫu (Câu 35) — 130 từ

> **The benefits of sharing household chores**
>
> Sharing household chores brings many benefits to a family. First, it makes the work lighter for everyone. When all members do their part, nobody has to spend too much time on cleaning, cooking or washing up, so parents have more time to rest after work. Second, sharing chores teaches children important life skills. They learn how to cook a simple meal, do the laundry and manage their time, which will be very useful when they live on their own. Third, working together strengthens family bonds. Parents and children can talk and laugh while they are washing up, so they understand each other better. Finally, children who do chores become more responsible and independent. In short, housework should be shared because it benefits both the family and each individual. *(131 words)*

**Vì sao bài này được điểm cao:**

| Tiêu chí | Biểu hiện trong bài |
|---|---|
| Đúng chủ đề, đủ ý | 4 ý: nhẹ việc – kỹ năng – gắn kết – trách nhiệm |
| Tổ chức rõ | Câu chủ đề → *First / Second / Third / Finally* → *In short* |
| Ngữ pháp đa dạng | hiện tại đơn, *when*, mệnh đề quan hệ *which/who*, *so* |
| Độ dài | 131 từ — nằm giữa khung 120–150 |

## 6.3. AUDIO SCRIPTS

### Script for Section A – Part 1

**Hoa:** Nam, do you have to help with the housework at home?

**Nam:** Yes, quite a lot. My mother works in the evening, so my sister and I have to do more than before.

**Hoa:** What do you usually do?

**Nam:** I take out the rubbish, wash the dishes and clean the floor. My sister waters the plants and does the laundry, but she's only fourteen, so she can't iron clothes.

**Hoa:** Does your father help?

**Nam:** He does the heavy lifting — cleaning the windows and fixing things around the house. He also cooks at the weekend. His pho is really good.

**Hoa:** Sounds fair. What about your grandparents?

**Nam:** They live with us. My grandfather takes my sister to school every morning, and my grandmother looks after the garden.

**Hoa:** So everyone does something. That sounds like a very well-organised family.

**Nam:** Exactly. My parents say that sharing chores teaches children to be responsible. I didn't like it before, but now I understand why they are right.

**Hoa:** I should start helping at home too. My mother does almost everything by herself, and I never really thought about it. Thanks for sharing, Nam.
