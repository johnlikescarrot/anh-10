## 4.7. Writing 1 – Re-ordering (2 câu)

### Bài tập 1 – Sắp xếp thành đoạn hội thoại

**Chọn phương án thể hiện đúng thứ tự của các câu sau để tạo thành một đoạn hội thoại hợp lý.**

(a) *Great! I'll bring my gloves and a big bag.*
(b) *Hi Phong, are you free this Saturday morning?*
(c) *We're going to collect rubbish along the river. Do you want to join us?*
(d) *I think so. Why?*
(e) *Yes, I'd love to. What should I bring?*

1. Which is the correct order?
A. b – d – c – e – a
B. b – c – d – e – a
C. d – b – c – a – e
D. b – d – e – c – a

**Đáp án:** [[KEY 1A]]

>+ **Vì sao**: (b) mở đầu bằng lời chào + câu hỏi → (d) trả lời "I think so. Why?" → (c) giải thích lý do và mời → (e) nhận lời + hỏi mang gì → (a) trả lời. **Cặp hỏi – đáp luôn đi liền nhau.**

### Bài tập 2 – Sắp xếp thành đoạn văn

**Sắp xếp các câu sau thành một đoạn văn mạch lạc.**

(a) *Secondly, it teaches them how to look after themselves when they leave home.*
(b) *In my opinion, children should share the housework in the family.*
(c) *For these reasons, sharing chores is good for both children and parents.*
(d) *First, doing chores together helps family members understand each other better.*

2. Which is the correct order?
A. b – a – d – c
B. d – a – b – c
C. b – d – c – a
D. b – d – a – c

**Đáp án:** [[KEY 2D]]

>+ **Vì sao**: (b) là **câu chủ đề** (nêu quan điểm) → (d) *First* → (a) *Secondly* → (c) *For these reasons* (câu kết). Dấu hiệu: các từ nối **First / Secondly / For these reasons** quyết định thứ tự.

### Bài tập 3 – Sắp xếp thành lá thư (nâng cao)

**Sắp xếp để tạo thành một bức thư điện tử hoàn chỉnh.**

(a) *I'm writing to tell you about our Go Green Club's clean-up day.*
(b) *Dear Mai,*
(c) *Best wishes,*
(d) *It will take place on Sunday morning, and we will meet at the school gate at 7 a.m.*
(e) *I hope you can join us. Please let me know before Friday.*
(f) *Nam*

3. Which is the correct order?
A. a – b – d – e – f – c
B. b – a – e – d – c – f
C. b – a – d – e – c – f
D. b – d – a – e – c – f

**Đáp án:** [[KEY 3C]]

>+ **Vì sao**: Lá thư luôn có **lời chào mở (b) → nội dung (a, d, e) → lời chào kết (c) → chữ ký (f)**. Trong phần nội dung, (a) nêu mục đích, (d) nêu thời gian địa điểm, (e) lời mời.

## 4.8. Writing 2 – Sentence transformation (12 câu)

**Viết lại các câu sau sao cho nghĩa không đổi, bắt đầu bằng từ/cụm từ cho sẵn.**

1. They clean the classroom every afternoon.
→ The classroom ________________________________________.

2. Someone stole my bike last night.
→ My bike ________________________________________.

3. The students are planting trees in the school yard now.
→ Trees ________________________________________.

4. We will organise a music show next Sunday.
→ A music show ________________________________________.

5. People believe that he is a talented composer.
→ It ________________________________________.

6. They made him work late yesterday.
→ He ________________________________________.

7. Although it rained heavily, we went to the concert.
→ It rained heavily, ________________________________________.

8. Because she was ill, she didn't go to school.
→ She was ill, ________________________________________.

9. It is important to share the housework.
→ Sharing ________________________________________.

10. I spend two hours doing my homework every day.
→ It takes ________________________________________.

11. My parents let me choose my own hobby.
→ My parents allow ________________________________________.

12. No one in my family cooks as well as my mother.
→ My mother ________________________________________.

**Đáp án:**

1. The classroom **is cleaned every afternoon**.
2. My bike **was stolen last night**.
3. Trees **are being planted in the school yard now**.
4. A music show **will be organised next Sunday**.
5. **It is believed that he is a talented composer.**
6. He **was made to work late yesterday**.
7. It rained heavily, **but we went to the concert**.
8. She was ill, **so she didn't go to school**.
9. Sharing **the housework is important**.
10. **It takes me two hours to do my homework every day.**
11. My parents allow **me to choose my own hobby**.
12. My mother **is the best cook in my family**.

>+ **Bảng tự chấm nhanh** – mỗi câu đúng cấu trúc được 0,25 điểm. Các lỗi bị trừ: quên **chia "be"**, quên **V3/ed**, quên **"to"** sau *be made*, dùng **but** sau *Although*, viết *Sharing the housework are important* (sai số ít/số nhiều).

## 4.9. Writing 3 – Viết đoạn văn (3 đề + bài mẫu)

**Viết một đoạn văn khoảng 120–150 từ cho mỗi đề sau.**

1. **Family life**: *What are the benefits of sharing the housework in the family?*
2. **Environment**: *What can students do to protect the environment?*
3. **Music**: *The role of music in your life.*

### Bài mẫu 1 – Family life (138 từ)

> In my opinion, sharing the housework brings three main benefits to a family. Firstly, it teaches children important life skills. When I learned to cook and do the laundry, I became much more independent, and I now feel ready to live alone when I go to university. Secondly, sharing chores reduces the burden on parents. Both of my parents work long hours, so when my brother and I tidy up the house, they have more time to rest. Finally, doing things together makes the family closer. Every Sunday morning, we clean the house as a team, and we always have lunch together afterwards. Those moments are when we talk and laugh the most. For these reasons, I strongly believe that every member of a family should take part in the housework, because a shared home is a happy home.

>+ **Phân tích bài mẫu**: câu chủ đề (câu 1) → *Firstly* → *Secondly* → *Finally* → *For these reasons* + câu kết. Đủ 5 từ nối, dùng 6 từ vựng chủ đề (*housework, laundry, burden, tidy up, chores*), đúng 138 từ.

### Bài mẫu 2 – Environment (142 từ)

> Students can do a lot to protect the environment, starting with small daily habits. First, we should reduce the amount of single-use plastic we use. I always carry a reusable water bottle and a cloth bag, and I refuse plastic straws when I buy drinks. Secondly, we can sort our rubbish into paper, plastic and organic waste so that it can be recycled more easily. Thirdly, joining a school club is a good way to make a real difference. Our Go Green Club organises a clean-up along the river every month, and last year we collected more than one tonne of rubbish. Finally, we should raise awareness among our friends and families by sharing simple tips on social media. In conclusion, protecting the environment does not require big actions; it requires millions of small ones, repeated every day.

>+ **Phân tích bài mẫu**: dùng *First / Secondly / Thirdly / Finally / In conclusion* – 5 từ nối; có **ví dụ cá nhân** (bình nước, câu lạc bộ) nên bài không bị "chung chung"; 142 từ.

### Bài mẫu 3 – Music (135 từ)

> Music plays a very important role in my life. Firstly, it helps me relax after a long day at school. Whenever I feel stressed before an exam, I put on my headphones and listen to a few favourite songs, and I always feel calmer afterwards. Secondly, music connects me with other people. My best friend and I both love Vietnamese pop, and we often go to live concerts together; those are some of my happiest memories. Finally, English songs are a fun way to learn the language. Thanks to the lyrics, I have learned many new words and improved my pronunciation without studying grammar rules. In short, music is not only entertainment for me. It is a friend, a teacher and a source of energy that makes every day better.

>+ **Phân tích bài mẫu**: 3 ý rõ ràng với *Firstly / Secondly / Finally*, câu kết mở rộng ấn tượng (*a friend, a teacher and a source of energy*), 135 từ.

### Khung viết "điền vào chỗ trống" – dùng khi bí ý

># **Câu 1 (chủ đề)**: *In my opinion, ______ plays an important role in ______.*
> **Câu 2 (ý 1)**: *Firstly, ______ helps me ______. For example, ______.*
> **Câu 3 (ý 2)**: *Secondly, ______. When I ______, I feel ______.*
> **Câu 4 (ý 3)**: *Finally, ______ because ______.*
> **Câu 5 (kết)**: *For these reasons, I believe that ______.*
> Điền 5 câu này + mỗi câu mở rộng thêm 1–2 câu giải thích là bạn có ngay **120–150 từ** đúng cấu trúc.
